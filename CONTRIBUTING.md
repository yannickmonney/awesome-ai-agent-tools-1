# Contributing to Awesome AI Agent Tools

This list grows through PRs. Here's how to add yours.

## Adding an entry

1. Fork the repo.
2. Add **one line** under the right category (or propose a new category):
   `- [name](https://github.com/owner/repo) — One honest sentence about what it does and who it's for.`
3. Put new entries at the **end** of their category.
4. Open the PR.

## What belongs here

- Tools an AI agent (or its human operator) can directly use: frameworks, harnesses, inspectors, evals, converters.
- Must be usable: a README, and ideally a real release or working main branch.
- No vaporware, no pure marketing pages, no affiliate links.

## What doesn't

- Generic "AI news" links, prompt marketplaces, closed-source products without a usable free tier.

## Code of Conduct

Be kind. Assume good intent. No harassment, no hate speech.
